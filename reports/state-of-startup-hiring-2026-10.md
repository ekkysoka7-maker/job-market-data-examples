# State of Startup Hiring: October 2026

*69,576 open jobs from the public job boards of tech startups, read directly from their applicant tracking systems (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workable, SmartRecruiters and others). Pay figures come only from postings that publish a range.*

## The numbers in one minute

- **Software Engineer (US):** median $210k, middle half $182k to $250k (2024 postings, 375 companies).
- **Best-paid common roles:** Research Engineer $325k, Research Scientist $274k, Group Product Manager $265k.
- **Pay bands are wide:** the typical gap between P25 and P75 is $40k.
- **Remote vs pay:** correlation 0.08 across US roles, so remote roles are not paid less.
- **Hiring bursts:** the 10 fastest-hiring startups posted 23% of the 10,767 new roles among accelerating companies (excluding large enterprises: Bosch Home Comfort, Pilot Company, AbbVie, Centria Autism).
- **ATS:** Greenhouse and Ashby host 71% of open startup jobs.

## Method

Jobs are read once a day from public job board endpoints. Pay is the midpoint of each published range; role and country groups need at least 5 postings from at least 3 companies. "New roles" counts still-open roles posted in the last 30 days. The sample leans to venture-backed tech companies, so it is not the whole market.
