# State of Startup Hiring: October 2026

*70,835 open jobs from the public job boards of tech startups, read directly from their applicant tracking systems (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workable, SmartRecruiters and others). Pay figures come only from postings that publish a range.*

## The numbers in one minute

- **Software Engineer (US):** median $211k, middle half $184k to $250k (2045 postings, 378 companies).
- **Best-paid common roles:** Research Engineer $325k, Director of Engineering $306k, Research Scientist $274k.
- **Pay bands are wide:** the typical gap between P25 and P75 is $40k.
- **Remote vs pay:** correlation 0.09 across US roles, so remote roles are not paid less.
- **Hiring bursts:** the 10 fastest-hiring startups posted 21% of the 12,418 new roles among accelerating companies (excluding large enterprises: Bosch Home Comfort, Pilot Company, AbbVie, Centria Autism).
- **ATS:** Greenhouse and Ashby host 70% of open startup jobs.

## Method

Jobs are read once a day from public job board endpoints. Pay is the midpoint of each published range; role and country groups need at least 5 postings from at least 3 companies. "New roles" counts still-open roles posted in the last 30 days. The sample leans to venture-backed tech companies, so it is not the whole market.
